Teacher Code of Conduct & Rules

Essential guidelines for all teaching faculty at GPS Dhok Jhatt

01

Punctuality & Morning Assembly

All teachers must arrive 15 minutes before assembly. Daily morning attendance must be logged before 08:30 AM.

02

Daily Syllabus Planning

Lesson plans and homework files must be uploaded to the portal at least 24 hours prior to class delivery.

03

Report Submission Deadline

Monthly student progress sheets must be submitted to the Head Teacher by the 28th of every calendar month.

04

Professional Conduct

Maintain respectful communication in peer discussion groups. Internal system files are confidential.

05

Meeting Attendance

Attendance is mandatory in staff meetings scheduled by Head Teacher or School Administrator.

06

Permission Hierarchy

Teachers cannot alter system ranks or change historical records once finalized by Head Teacher or Admin.
